FLOOR 03 / TRAINING

Learn the craft.

Boxing has a language. We teach it one useful movement at a time, with room to make mistakes and ask questions.

A GOOD PLACE TO START

The fundamentals first.

01 / POSITION

Stance & movement

Find your balance, move without crossing your feet and keep a position you can defend from.

02 / CONNECTION

Jab & cross

Build straight punches from the floor up. Learn the distance, the rotation and the return to guard.

03 / AWARENESS

Defence & timing

See what is coming, make space and put the pieces together through careful partner drills.

YOUR FIRST SESSION

No experience
required.

You’ll meet a coach, learn your stance and try the first few movements at your own pace. There’s no sparring in your first session. Wear comfortable clothes, bring water, and arrive a little early.
